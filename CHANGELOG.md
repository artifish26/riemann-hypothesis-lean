# Changelog

## [0.3.0] - 2026-08-25

- Added the production Hardy H0--H4D chain and the checked endpoint proving
  that infinitely many actual nontrivial zeta zeros lie on the critical line.
- Added X19B and the source-package-free Suzuki S4 publication wrapper,
  including the canonical compact-window solution family, uniform norm bound
  `4000`, Lipschitz spectral dependence, compact image, and directed radius
  continuity from below.
- Published S4 with a focused claim ledger, axiom audit, adversarial Lean
  consumer, and independent scope report; the programme is frozen at the
  compact-window result.
- Added the audited RIG0--RIG1 paper result: no objectwise fixed-zero transport
  and a quantitative annular escape estimate on a hypothetical complete
  first-crossing kernel. This result is explicitly not formalised in Lean.
- Recorded the `DO NOT ADMIT RIG2` decision because the canonical annular
  complement remains uncontrolled and generally infinite-dimensional.
- Added a headline-based version history to the README.
- Kept all-radius Suzuki nondegeneracy, global Li/Weil positivity, and RH
  explicitly open.
